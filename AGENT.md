# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition `u-agentfid001-the-dog-ate-my-homework-funniest-excuse-8659c98c`
("The Dog Ate My Homework: Funniest Excuse").

- `main.py` — stdlib-only entrypoint. Holds the excuse as a constant and
  writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output/entry.txt`),
  with no trailing newline so the gate counts exactly the line itself.
- The line: a dog caught eating the homework explains itself in its own
  voice — it took "show your work" literally and ate the proof, so now
  nobody can say the homework wasn't done, and nobody can say it was.
  The twist is dog logic with zero embarrassment: the dog believes it
  did nothing wrong. It is fresh (no cat, no taste of paper, no good boy)
  and works standalone with no setup.
- `README.md` — bounty badges (new make.fun badge format) and the line.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
